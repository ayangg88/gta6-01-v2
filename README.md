# MERIDIAN HEAT

Take the wheel as Rae Delgado, a former getaway driver trying to make a name in Port Meridian, a fictional harbor city built for 2D top-down crime and exploration. Walk the streets or drive through traffic and crowds, commandeer cars, motorcycles, and boats, and see how long you can stay ahead of the law as your wanted level climbs from zero to five stars. Police response grows from officers and cruisers to roadblocks, helicopters, and armored vans.

Eight story jobs pair missions across the city with character dialogue, from waterfront fares and races to robberies and a harbor heist. Get into combat with a pistol, shotgun, or SMG, and visit the arms dealer for gear. Earn cash through robberies and taxi work, build respect, and spend at the garage. Away from the main jobs, search for hidden packages, hit stunt jumps, and race. A minimap HUD helps you find your way around the changing day and night, with rain, synthesized WebAudio effects, and three radio stations along for the ride. Progress is saved in your browser's localStorage.

## Run-it

With Node.js 22, start the included local server from the repository root:

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open [http://localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080).
